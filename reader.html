The Wrong Groom

Oops, I Married the Wrong Man

Mia Adams stood beneath the wedding lights, her heart pounding as she stared at the man beside her.

Something was wrong.

This wasn't Daniel.

The man holding her hand was Alexander Carter, Daniel's older brother—the quiet, intimidating man she had barely spoken to before today.

Mia's fingers trembled. “Where is Daniel?”

Alexander's expression remained calm. “That's what I'm trying to find out.”

The room seemed to spin. Her wedding dress felt suddenly too heavy, and every whisper from the guests sounded like a warning.

Before Mia could move, the officiant placed the marriage certificate in front of them.

Her name was already there.

Alexander's name was already there too.

Then Alexander leaned closer and lowered his voice.

“Someone wanted you to marry me.”

Mia stared at him in disbelief.

It looked like a trap.
