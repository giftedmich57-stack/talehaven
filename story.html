Oops, I Married the Wrong Man

Mia Adams was ready to marry Daniel Carter. But at the altar, the man beside her wasn't Daniel. It was Alexander Carter—Daniel's mysterious older brother.

Alexander knew she wasn't his intended bride. Yet somehow, the marriage certificate was already signed.

Then Alexander looked at Mia and said, “Someone wanted you to marry me.”

What was supposed to be a perfect wedding had become a dangerous mystery.
